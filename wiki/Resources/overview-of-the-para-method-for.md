# The PARA Method Overview

Organize digital information into 4 top-level categories:
1. **Projects**: Short-term efforts with deadline and goal.
2. **Areas**: Ongoing responsibilities to maintain standard.
3. **Resources**: Topics of ongoing interest or reference.
4. **Archives**: Inactive items from the other 3 categories.
